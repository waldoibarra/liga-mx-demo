Rules, sources, and model limitations
  • Eight clubs qualify directly. Quarterfinal seeds pair 1–8, 2–7, 3–6, and 4–5.
  • Semifinalists are reseeded using their regular-season positions.
  • All rounds use two legs. In quarterfinals and semifinals, the higher seed advances on aggregate ties.
  • Final aggregate ties go to two 15-minute periods, then penalties if necessary.
  • Penalty kicks alternate. The shootout ends early if one side cannot catch up.
  • After five kicks each, tied shootouts continue until one side leads after an equal number of kicks.

The simulator holds the official table fixed. It assumes clubs remain eligible and higher seeds host second legs.

It models tournament outcomes, not player eligibility, disciplinary events, administrative exceptions, or kickoff assignments.

Goals use independent Poisson draws. Extra time uses one-third of each club’s second-leg rate.

Each penalty has an assumed 75% conversion rate. No injury, lineup, or expected-goals data is used.

The club model performed worse than league averages on a 36-match holdout. Treat outputs as scenarios, not calibrated forecasts.
